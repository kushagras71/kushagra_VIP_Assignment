# Seasonal Buy Windows — Branded Merchandise

This reference defines the planning windows distributors use to commit branded merchandise orders to suppliers. These windows are aligned with the supplier production calendar — orders placed outside the window may face extended lead times or be rejected.

## Window definitions

| Window Code | Category | Order-by Week | Delivery Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SS-EARLY | Apparel (Spring/Summer) | Week 5 | Weeks 12–14 | Locked allocation, no late-buys |
| SS-LATE | Apparel (Spring/Summer) | Week 14 | Weeks 18–22 | Reorders permitted, smaller MOQ |
| HEADWEAR-SS | Headwear (Summer) | Week 8 | Weeks 16–20 | |
| FW-EARLY | Apparel (Fall/Winter) | Week 28 | Weeks 35–38 | Includes hoodies, flannels |
| FW-LATE | Apparel (Fall/Winter) | Week 36 | Weeks 41–44 | Reorders only |
| DRINKWARE-Q4 | Drinkware (Holiday) | Week 30 | Weeks 40–44 | Critical window — late buys penalised |
| DRINKWARE-NONHOL | Drinkware (non-holiday) | Open | Rolling | Limited MOQ |
| ACCESSORIES | Accessories (year-round) | Open | Rolling | No window restrictions |

## MOQ (minimum order quantity) by window

- Apparel SS-EARLY: 250 units per SKU per cluster
- Apparel SS-LATE: 100 units per SKU per cluster
- Headwear summer window: 200 units per SKU per cluster
- FW-EARLY apparel: 300 units per SKU per cluster (heavier garments)
- DRINKWARE-Q4: 500 units per SKU per cluster (highest MOQ — Q4 demand)
- Accessories: 100 units per SKU, no cluster split required

## Late-buy rules

A "late buy" is any order placed after the window's order-by week. Late buys are subject to:

1. A 15% surcharge from the supplier (passed through to the distributor).
2. Lead time extension of 4–6 weeks beyond the standard delivery window.
3. No guarantee of allocation — late buys are filled after committed orders are satisfied.

## Standing orders

Standing orders (recurring weekly or biweekly auto-replenishment) are **not permitted for branded merchandise**. Suppliers require a per-window commitment with a defined quantity. Distributors found to be operating informal standing orders with retailers must convert these to per-window commits at the start of the next planning cycle.
