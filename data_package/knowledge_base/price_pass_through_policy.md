# Price Pass-Through Policy

How wholesale-price changes from suppliers should flow through to the distributor's retail price for branded merchandise SKUs.

## Default rule

Wholesale price increases from the supplier are passed through to retail at the same margin percentage the distributor was operating before the change. The dollar markup adjusts to preserve the margin.

Example: a SKU at $10 wholesale with a 40% margin sells at $16.67. If the supplier raises the wholesale to $11, the new retail under default pass-through is $18.33 (still 40% margin), not $17.67 (which would be the same $6.67 dollar markup).

## Exceptions

Pass-through is **not** automatic in these cases:

1. **Promotional windows.** During an active supplier-funded promo, the retail price is held flat for the duration of the promo, regardless of any wholesale change. The wholesale change applies after the promo window closes.

2. **Specialty retailers.** Specialty retailers negotiate their own margin percentages and are not bound by the default pass-through formula.

3. **End-of-life inventory.** If a SKU is in its end-of-life window, the retail price is governed by the EOL drawdown policy, not pass-through.

4. **Inventory acquired before the change.** Pre-change inventory continues at the pre-change retail price until exhausted. Distributors must FIFO their stock to comply.

## Communication requirements

Any wholesale price change requires a minimum 30-day notice from the supplier. Distributors must communicate the resulting retail change to their retailers no less than 14 days before the change takes effect.

## Audit

The Category Management team audits a sample of pass-through events each quarter. Distributors with margin compression beyond the auto-tolerance band (margin drops more than 3 percentage points from baseline) are flagged for review.
