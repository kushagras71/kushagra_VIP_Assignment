# Planogram Policy — Apparel

Memo to all distributors carrying branded apparel SKUs in the FY26 cycle.

The planogram policy for apparel was revised in February 2026 to reflect the move toward seasonal merchandising blocks. The summary below is binding for any retailer carrying our co-branded apparel range.

## Minimum on-shelf presence

Each apparel SKU placed in-store must maintain a minimum of four units across the available size range during the active selling window. Below this threshold, retailers are required to consolidate to a single representative SKU or remove the category from the planogram.

## Seasonal windows

Apparel is treated as a seasonal category. The active selling windows are:

1. **Spring/Summer window:** weeks 14 through 36. Heavier tee and tank inventory expected.
2. **Fall/Winter window:** weeks 37 through 5 (wrapping the year). Long sleeves, hoodies, flannels.

Outside these windows, apparel SKUs default to the clearance rail and the safety stock policy is suspended.

## Size-mix requirement

For every apparel SKU, the retailer must carry a minimum of three size variants. The recommended mix is:

- S: 15%
- M: 30%
- L: 30%
- XL: 20%
- XXL: 5%

This mix is a starting point. Cluster-level adjustments are permitted based on prior-year sell-through, but must be documented in the buy plan and approved by the Category Manager.

## Display requirements

Co-branded apparel may not be displayed within 18 inches of competing brands. Where retailer shelf space requires adjacency, the supplier's display kit must be used to delineate the product range.

## Exceptions

Specialty retailers (defined in the cluster_definitions reference) may operate on alternative planograms with prior approval. Standard retailers may not.
