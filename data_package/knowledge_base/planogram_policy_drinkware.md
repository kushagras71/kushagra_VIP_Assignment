# Planogram — Drinkware

Effective: Jan 2026

- All drinkware SKUs must be displayed on backlit or front-lit shelving where available.
- Minimum facing count: 2 per SKU during the Q4 window (weeks 40–52), 1 per SKU otherwise.
- Glassware SKUs (pint glass, pilsner, highball, coupe) require breakage-rate handling: assume 4% loss in distributor warehouse and an additional 2% in retailer transit.
- Insulated drinkware (tumbler, flask) must include the supplier's care card at point of sale. Cards are supplied free of charge with each pallet.
- Coffee mugs and travel mugs may be cross-merchandised with coffee-program retailers; in this case the planogram defers to the retailer's beverage program.
- Holiday window (weeks 47–52): drinkware moves to endcap or co-merchandised display with the parent beverage SKU. Funding for endcap placement is supplier-paid per the promo calendar.
- Stemless wine glasses are restricted to the Crestmark Bourbon and Black Pine Whiskey programs only — they are not part of the general drinkware planogram.

## Pricing display

The MSRP must be visible. Where the retailer applies a custom price, the supplier's MSRP must still be shown on the unit packaging or via a shelf tag.

## Returns

Glassware returns are restricted: only unopened cases may be returned, and only within 14 days of receipt. After that, the unit is considered consumed and any unsold inventory is the distributor's responsibility.
