## Notes on co-marketing windows

Co-marketing windows are negotiated 90+ days in advance with the supplier marketing team. The funding percentage shown above is the supplier's contribution to the markdown — distributors are responsible for the residual. **A 60% co-marketing rate means the supplier rebates 60% of the unit margin loss during the promo window**, not 60% of the SKU cost.

## Historical funding rates by promo type (for planning)

For SKUs without a specific funding rate confirmed yet, use these averages:

- TPR: 30–35% funding
- Display: 20–25% funding
- Co-Marketing: 50–60% funding
- BOGO: not funding-based — supplier replenishes free units 1:1

## Coordination

For any clarification on calendar entries, the contact is the Category Management lead for the relevant parent brand. Calendar entries are firm once they appear in the "Confirmed" table — suppliers very rarely cancel inside a 60-day window, but if they do, the distributor is held harmless on already-committed inventory provided the commitment was placed through the standard ABC Planning workflow.
